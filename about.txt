# Lebanopoly – A Lebanese Twist on Monopoly

**Lebanopoly** is a Lebanese-themed adaptation of the classic Monopoly board game. Created by Lebanese designer **Toni Jammal**, it aims to celebrate Lebanon’s cultural heritage by integrating well-known landmarks, nostalgic currency, and traditional elements into gameplay.

## 🎲 Key Features

- **🇱🇧 Lebanese Landmarks**  
  The game features **28 notable locations** from across Lebanon — including historical castles, towns, and cultural sites — allowing players to virtually explore the country’s diverse regions.

- **💰 Vintage Currency**  
  Players use **replica Lebanese lira notes** from denominations of 1 to 1000, evoking nostalgia for Lebanon in the 1990s.

- **🧿 Cultural Cards**  
  Includes **“fortune-telling”** and **“town square”** cards with traditional Lebanese themes, adding humor and cultural flavor to the game.

- **📚 Educational Value**  
  Great for all ages, Lebanopoly is both fun and informative — helping players learn about Lebanese geography, culture, and history.

## 🛒 Availability

- Sold at stores across Lebanon: **ABC Achrafieh**, **ABC Dbayeh**, **Hazmiyeh**, **Hamra**, and **Sin El-Fil**.
- Suitable for ages **8+** and for **2 to 8 players**.

## 🧍 Life-Sized Version

For an immersive experience, you can try the **life-sized Lebanopoly** at **Funloc in Amchit**, where players walk around a giant board and physically engage with the spaces.

## 🌍 Summary

Whether you’re a local or a visitor, **Lebanopoly** is a unique, entertaining way to connect with Lebanon’s rich cultural tapestry — all through a game that’s as educational as it is enjoyable.
